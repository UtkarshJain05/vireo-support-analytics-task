## Executive Finding

Your CSAT has dropped from **3.49 to 3.25** (out of 5) since the festive season, driven by a surge in ticket volume and a sharp rise in product replacements. Ten Tier 1 agents have been flagged for priority review. However, eight of the ten work in Logistics and Returns — queues that handle structurally harder cases by design. Training should focus on those queues and the specific issue patterns driving dissatisfaction, not solely on individual agents.

---

## What Is Happening

- Ticket volume spiked roughly 30% during the festive season (Q4 2025) and replacement rates more than doubled — from about 10% pre-festive to 25% in Q1 2026. They have since eased to about 17%, but remain well above the earlier baseline.
- Average handle time rose from roughly 22 hours to over 35 hours in Q1 2026 as the desk absorbed the backlog. It has since improved but remains elevated.
- CSAT fell most sharply in January–February 2026 (to around 2.95) and has partially recovered to about 3.40 by June 2026.
- Replacement spend went from roughly ₹1.6–1.8 lakh per quarter (early 2025) to nearly ₹14.8 lakh in Q1 2026.

## Major Observed Drivers and Themes

1. **Delivery & Shipping issues** dominate the flagged agents' queues (about 50% of their tickets). Lost-in-transit and delayed shipments are the primary complaints.
2. **Returns & Refunds** account for roughly 28% of flagged agents' volume. Customers frequently express frustration with the return process timeline.
3. **Product quality concerns** — especially around audio issues, charging/battery problems, and connectivity — appear frequently in low-CSAT tickets across all teams.
4. **The replacement rate doubling** correlates with festive-season sales volume. This may involve lot-level product quality issues, not just support handling.

---

## Proposed Training Focus

1. **Delivery escalation protocols** — proactive communication with customers when tracking stalls; faster courier escalation processes.
2. **Empathy and de-escalation** — especially for Logistics and Returns agents handling frustrated customers with undelivered or defective products.
3. **First-contact resolution techniques** — reducing the need for transfers and follow-up contacts.
4. **Product troubleshooting** — particularly audio quality, connectivity, and charging issues, so agents can resolve more cases without issuing replacements.
5. **Quality of resolution** — for the two Chat Frontline agents with fast but low-rated interactions: spending a few more minutes to ensure the customer's actual problem is addressed.

## Quantified Business Objective

**Goal:** Reduce the replacement rate from the current approximately 22% back toward the pre-festive baseline of approximately 10%.

**Estimated quarterly impact:** Approximately **₹8.5 lakh per quarter** in avoided replacement costs.

**Calculation basis:**
- Average replacement cost: approximately ₹1,800 per unit (product unit cost + ₹340 shipping, per the support operating policy).
- Recent quarters average roughly ₹10.5 lakh in replacement costs versus roughly ₹2.0 lakh during the pre-festive baseline.
- The difference represents the savings opportunity if replacement rates return to baseline levels.

**Training budget:** ₹4 lakh for Q3 — this investment would pay for itself within one quarter if even half the replacement rate reduction is achieved.

## Important Caveats

- The replacement spike may partly reflect product quality or lot-level defects rather than agent behaviour. Investigating specific product SKUs and lot codes with the warehouse team (Rohan) would help distinguish the two causes.
- CSAT response rate is about 44%. Scores are based on the roughly 5,200 customers who responded; non-respondents may differ.
- Tier 2 agents (Escalations & Warranty, 6 agents) were excluded from the bottom-10 ranking because their work is structurally different — multi-touch warranty cases measured on resolution days, not tickets closed.
- Handle time for legacy tickets (pre-September 2025) was reconstructed from event logs and may be less precise than current helpdesk data.
- The ₹8.5 lakh estimate assumes training and process changes can fully close the gap. Actual results will depend on whether root causes are within the agents' control.
